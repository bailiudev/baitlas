ABOUT BAITLAS

Every story has a place.

Baitlas brings maps, timelines, and field notes together. Follow a journey, open a place, and explore the sources behind the story.

This first edition follows Lewis and Clark. Read and Hike share the same story view. The sample map is historical interpretation and does not provide hiking directions.
