ABOUT BAITLAS

Every story
has a place.

Baitlas is an atlas for following your curiosity.

A map gives a story somewhere to happen. A timeline gives it a rhythm. Field notes leave room for the people, details, and questions along the way.

Two ways into the atlas

Read collects stories and expeditions. Hike looks at journeys through their routes and landscapes. Both bring you to the same kind of article: a map to explore, moments to follow, and notes to read.

Begin with a journey west

The first story follows the Lewis & Clark Expedition. Historical sources accompany the account, and approximate geography is identified on the map. The historical route study is not a modern hiking guide.
